Shadowday and UAE schools

  • Since the 2025–26 school year, AI has been a required subject in every UAE public school, from kindergarten to Grade 12.1
  • Dubai-based companies raised about 98% of UAE tech funding in the first nine months of 2025, by Tracxn's data.2
  • Children who grow up around inventors are much more likely to become inventors.3

A student in Liwa, Dibba or Kalba learns AI in class but never sees an engineer's day. Shadowday shows one, and lets them do it.

Can I use Shadowday in Arabic?

Yes. Every screen works in Arabic, right to left.

Why ask the customer questions?

Each customer knows a rule the ticket leaves out, like Saeed's 20% limit.

Does Ravi write my code?

No. He answers with questions, so the code you push is yours.

Do I join Falaj Labs, WadiWatch or Barid Labs?

You join the one in the area you pick: Liwa puts you at Falaj Labs.

Is Falaj Labs a real startup?

No. All three startups are made up; the problems they work on are real.

Can teachers use it with their students?

Yes. Students open it in a browser; the school sets nothing up.
